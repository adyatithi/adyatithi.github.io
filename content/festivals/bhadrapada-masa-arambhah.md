`bhAdrapada-mAsaH` begins today --- As stated in the Mahābhārata, a person who observes the month with the discipline of eating only once a day (ekāhāra) attains immense and incomparable wealth, beginning with riches and prosperity. In the Vāmana Purāṇa, it is said that during Bhādrapada, one should make offerings to please Hṛṣīkeśa (Viṣṇu) --— this includes donating iron, ghee mixed with honey, salt, and jaggery-rice (guḍodana).
