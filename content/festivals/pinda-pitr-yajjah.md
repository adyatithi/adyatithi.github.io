description_en = '''
Pinda Pitru Yajnam is a `zrAddham` to be done to two sets of Pitru Devatas. It is to be done in one's Agnihotra Agni or if that is not present then Aupasana Agni. Rice is cooked in a section of the Agni moved to the south-east. From this, even those whose father is living, give `Ahuti` to the Deva Pitru-s ie `sOma`, `yama` and `agni kavyavAhana`. Those whose father is not living should additionally give `piNDa`s to the Manushya Pitru-s also to whom `amAvAsyA-zrAddham` is also performed.
