description_en = '''
It is important to have darshana of the Moon at start of every lunar month, but it is more special in `bhAdrapada` month, preceding `zrIvinAyaka-caturthI`. As said in the `syamantakOpAkhyAnam`, if one performs chandra-darshana before the `caturthI` day, they do not accrue any `dOSa` from chandra-darshana on the chaturthi day! Have darshan of Moon today, chanting the latter shloka given.
'''
