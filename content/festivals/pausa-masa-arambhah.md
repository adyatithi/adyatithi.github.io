description_en = '''
`pauSa-mAsaH` begins today --- In the Mahābhārata, it is said that one who observes the month of Pauṣa with the discipline of ekabhakta (eating only once a day) becomes charming, pleasing to behold, and attains lasting fame. In the Vāmana Purāṇa, it is stated that during Pauṣa, one should diligently donate buildings, towns, and household furnishings (gṛha-prāvaraṇāni) to please Nārāyaṇa.
