Perform snana four ghatikas before sunrise (during `aruNodayaH`, when the Moon is still up, on the full-moon days of `ASADha`, `kArttika`, `mAgha` and `vaizAkha` – hence the name `A-kA-mA-vai`) bestows strength, beauty, fame, dharma, knowledge, happiness, fortitude and health.
