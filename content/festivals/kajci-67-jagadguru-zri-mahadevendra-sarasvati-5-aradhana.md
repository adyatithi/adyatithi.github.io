description_en = '''
Preceptor by name Mahādevendra adorning Pīṭha attained siddhi on the first day (pratipat) of bright fortnight of Phālguna month in the year Parābhava. The name of this preceptor before initiation was Lakṣmikānta. His father was well-known as Narasimhaśāstri. This preceptor was well-versed in Ṛgveda. His place of sidhi was Kalavai village in Śalivahana era 1830. The adhishthanam is at the Kalavai Brindavanam, at https://maps.app.goo.gl/j4cKLo3CWWJeBFJC9.
