description_en = '''
`sAmba`, the son of Sri Krishna, was cured of leprosy after performing `tapaH` to propitiate Surya Bhagavan. `sAmba` was the son of Bhagavan Sri Krishna and Jambavan's daughter, Jambavati. He was afflicted with leprosy owing to some sins accumulated in previous births. Deeply remorseful, he approached his father, Sri Krishna. Bhagavan pointed out that there was no other recourse to curing this disease other than the `upAsana` of `sUrya bhagavAn` and narrated the method to propitiate Surya Bhagavan and the story of a `caNDAla strI` who had gotten cured from disease by Surya Bhagavan. `sAmba` then propitiated Surya Bhagavan with bhakti. Pleased with his devotion, Surya Bhagavan revealed to him the `AryAdvAdazakam` stuti (http://stotrasamhita.net/wiki/Dwadasharya_Surya_Stuti). Performing `pArAyaNam` of this stuti, `sambA` was cured of this debilitating disease.
