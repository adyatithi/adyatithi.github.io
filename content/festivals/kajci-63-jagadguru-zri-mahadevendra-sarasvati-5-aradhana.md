Preceptor Śrī Mahādevendra governed the preceptorship for thirty-one years, attained siddhi on Śukla Dvādaśī (twelfth day of bright fortnight) of the month Āṣāḍha in the year Śrīmukha. His place of siddhi was Kumbhaghona town in Śalivahana era 1736. The adhishthanam is located at https://maps.app.goo.gl/Zrry6C2Sbqd3NUAS6, in the premises of the Kumbhaghonam Kanchi Shankara Matham.
