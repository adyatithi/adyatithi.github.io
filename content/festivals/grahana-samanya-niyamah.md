General notes: Avoid touching anything other than what's needed for the anushthana (especially bed/mat/cloth) until the mokSha snAna after the eclipse ends -- this is grahaNa Ashaucham. Pregnant women should avoid exposure to the sun/moon's light during the respective eclipse.
