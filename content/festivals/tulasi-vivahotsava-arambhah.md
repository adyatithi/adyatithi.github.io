description_en = '''
This day marks the beginning of a three-day festival culminating in Tulasi Vivaha. One should observe the Tulasī marriage festival according to the specific directives of their Vedic tradition. There is no doubt that participating in this event is as meritorious as performing a `kanyAdAnam`. On the bright fortnight's ninth day of Kārttika month, one should craft a golden `pratimA` of Hari and Tulasī, and worship it. This Vrata should be observed for three days, encompassing the rites of marriage as prescribed. These three days must include the Navamī day. If Navamī coincides with the previous Aṣṭamī day, the observance extends to midday. Furthermore, planting Dhātrī and Aśvattha trees together and celebrating this festival ensures enduring merits through hundres of crores of Kalpas!
'''
