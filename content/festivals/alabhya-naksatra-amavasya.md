description_en = '''
It is said in Vishnu Purāṇam that when Amavasya overlaps with one of nine nakshatras—Anuradha, Vishakha, Swati, Pushya, Ardra, Punarvasu, Shravishtha, Purvaproshthapada, or Shatabhishak—it is sacred even for Devas. For instance, Shraaddha done on an Amavasya joined with Anuradha, Vishakha, Swati keeps the `pitr`s satisfied for eight years. Likewise, on Pushya, Ardra and Punarvasu, the `pitr`s are satisfied for twelve years.
'''
